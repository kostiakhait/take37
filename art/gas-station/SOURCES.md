# Gas station: sources

Built by `build_gas.py` (Blender 4.2, headless) with the helpers in `art/street/` (`kitlib.py`, `props_kit.py`). No photographs, no external models, no brand: pumps, canopy, price pole
and shop are generic 1980s American forms in neutral house colours (white, red band); the price pole is blank-headed and carries three generic grade rows.

## Borrowed from the asset library (заимствовано из Феррета)

Tinted in `art/street/textures_prep.py`, maps themselves unedited:

| Library texture | Used for |
|---|---|
| tex-concrete-block | forecourt slab, island kerbs, perimeter kerb, column plinths |
| tex-brick-clay-new-material-sphere-2 (buff firing), tex-brick-clay-old | shop and car-wash walls |
| tex-metal-steel | painted pump cabinets, canopy fascia, columns, price pole, air stand (tinted, non-metal), bare steel parts |
| tex-metal-black | pump bases, door frames, black iron |
| tex-stucko-wa1, tex-column-plaster-311 | canopy soffit, shop interior walls, trim |
| tex-wood-oak, tex-wood-dark-brown | shop floor and counter |
| tex-ground-grass | strip behind the lot |

## Made for the game

Forecourt decals (oil and fuel stains, cracks, patches, drain grates, manhole covers; from the street decal atlas), shop interior backdrop, all geometry and the vertex-tone weathering.
Typeface in textures: DejaVu (free licence).
